Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

Stratto Coffee & Co. respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect at our café and through this website, and how we look after it.

What we collect

Only what you share when you call, message or book through WhatsApp, Instagram or email: your name, phone number, email and booking details. Our café may use CCTV for safety. Cash, UPI and card payments are handled by your bank or payment provider, and we never store card details or UPI PINs. This website has no sign-up or checkout; our hosting provider may log basic technical data such as your IP address to keep it running.

How we use it

To reply to you, confirm your visit, keep the café safe and meet legal duties such as GST billing. We send offers only with your consent and you can opt out any time. We never sell your data.

Who we share it with

Only service providers we rely on (payments, hosting, messaging) or authorities when the law requires. Google Maps, Instagram and WhatsApp follow their own privacy policies.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable safeguards and keep data only as long as needed, or as long as Indian tax and business law requires. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sharing sensitive details in messages.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance. Write to mkcafe1525@gmail.com and we will respond promptly.

Children

If you are under 18, please involve a parent or guardian before sharing personal details with us.

Changes and law

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
